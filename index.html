Why “instar”?
An instar is the stage an aquatic insect lives in between molts. A dragonfly nymph spends years underwater, growing through instar after instar, shedding a skin each time it outgrows the last, until it finally emerges. Each instar here is the same: you stay until the work is done — every experience checked, every skill performing, every gate excelling — and only then molt into the next. Certifications are a card; an instar is earned.
How grading works
Developing — completes the skill with coaching, hesitation, or visible corrections.
Performing — completes it independently and to standard, repeatably across dives.
Excelling — to standard under task load or changing conditions, without hesitation; could demonstrate it for a teammate.

Where a skill has a measurable standard, tap “Target” under it to see the depth, angle, time or distance for each level.

A instar is complete when every experience is checked, every supporting skill is Performing or better, and every performance gate is Excelling. Tap a SKILL/EXP tag to switch how an item is tracked.
